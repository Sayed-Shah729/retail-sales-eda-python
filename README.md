#  Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project performed on a retail sales dataset using Python. This project explores sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, and regional trends through statistical analysis and data visualization.

---

##  Project Overview

The objective of this project is to explore and analyse a retail sales dataset containing **9,994 records** and **21 features**. The analysis focuses on understanding sales performance, identifying profitable business areas, evaluating customer and product trends, and presenting insights using clear visualizations.

The project demonstrates practical data analysis techniques commonly used in business intelligence and entry-level data analyst roles.

---

##  Project Objectives

- Explore and understand the retail sales dataset.
- Inspect data quality by checking missing values and duplicate records.
- Generate descriptive statistics for key business metrics.
- Analyse sales, profit, quantity, and discount.
- Compare sales performance across regions and product categories.
- Identify top-performing cities, customers, and products.
- Visualize sales trends and business performance.
- Summarize key findings from the analysis.

##  Key Findings

- The **West** region generated the highest total sales.
- The **South** region recorded the lowest sales.
- Approximately **18.7%** of all orders resulted in a financial loss.
- **New York City** recorded the highest sales among all cities.
- The project identified the **Top 5 Customers** based on total sales.
- The project identified the **Top 5 Most Profitable Products**.
- **November 2017** recorded the highest monthly sales, while **February 2014** recorded the lowest monthly sales.
